 #  Retail Sales & Customer Intelligence Using PostgreSQL Business Insights 

## 1. Product Sales vs. Profitability

**Mobile Phones generated the highest revenue of KSh 6,942,060.65**, followed by Computing at KSh 6,596,221.70. However, Accessories generated the highest profit margin at approximately **42.39%**, despite generating lower revenue of KSh 1,077,496.10.
High sales revenue does not always translate into the highest profitability. Mobile Phones contribute significantly to revenue, while Accessories provide a much stronger profit margin.
Maintain strong sales and stock availability for Mobile Phones and Computing, while increasing the promotion and distribution of Accessories to improve overall profitability.

## 2. Revenue Performance by City
Nairobi is the leading market, generating **KSh 9,581,980.35**, representing approximately **39.67% of total revenue**. Mombasa followed with KSh 3,161,889.60, while Nakuru generated KSh 2,391,328.00. Machakos recorded the lowest revenue at KSh 745,099.90.
Revenue is concentrated in a few locations, particularly Nairobi. This indicates that some markets contribute more to business performance than others.
Maintain strong customer relationships and product availability in Nairobi and Mombasa. Investigate opportunities to increase distribution, marketing and customer acquisition in lower-performing markets such as Machakos.

## 3. Customer Segment Performance
Retail customers generated the highest revenue of **KSh 14,394,618.20**, supported by 88 customers.
Corporate customers generated KSh 4,948,967.75 from 25 customers, while VIP customers generated KSh 4,811,785.75 from 19 customers.
Corporate customers recorded the highest profit margin at approximately **15.25%**, compared with 11.70% for Retail and 9.40% for VIP customers.
Retail customers provide the largest revenue contribution, but Corporate customers deliver stronger profitability. VIP customers generate substantial revenue but have a relatively lower margin.
Continue growing the Retail customer base, develop more Corporate accounts and review discounts or service costs offered to VIP customers to improve their profitability.

## 4. Customers at Risk of Becoming Inactive
It  identify **65 out of 150 customers (43.33%)** as dormant. These customers had no completed purchase within the 90 days preceding the latest order date in the dataset, or had never made a completed purchase.
A large inactive customer base may indicate customer retention challenges and potential lost revenue. Acquiring new customers can also require more effort than retaining existing ones.
Introduce customer reactivation campaigns, targeted offers and follow-up communication. Prioritise previously active customers with high historical spending and monitor repeat purchases after each campaign.

## 5. Monthly Revenue Growth and Seasonality
The monthly sales analysis shows that revenue increased from **KSh 4,871,694.45 in Q2 2026 to KSh 5,862,270.55 in Q3 2026**, representing approximately **20.33% growth**.
August 2026 recorded the highest monthly revenue in the 2026 data at **KSh 2,242,033.90**. December 2025 was the strongest month in 2025, generating KSh 2,027,120.60.
Additionally, revenue from January to September 2026 was approximately 86.31% higher than the same period in 2025.
The results indicate improving revenue performance and possible seasonal sales peaks. December and the middle-to-later months of 2026 recorded particularly strong sales.
Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns ahead of historically strong periods. Continue monitoring monthly revenue and profit to determine whether growth is sustainable and to identify the products driving seasonal demand.

## 6. Customer Value Bands
Customers were grouped according to their total completed-order spending:

- **High-value customers (KSh 500,000 and above):** 16 customers generated KSh 10,621,517.45, approximately 43.97% of total revenue.
- **Medium-value customers (KSh 100,000–499,999):** 43 customers generated KSh 10,310,152.85, approximately 42.68% of total revenue.
- **Low-value customers (below KSh 100,000):** 91 customers generated KSh 3,223,701.40, approximately 13.35% of total revenue.
 Although high-value customers represent only 10.67% of the customer base, they contribute nearly 44% of total revenue. Medium-value customers also provide a significant revenue contribution and may have potential to become high-value customers.

**Recommendation:** 
Prioritise retention of high-value customers through loyalty programmes and personalised service. Encourage medium-value customers to increase their spending through targeted promotions and cross-selling. 
Use affordable offers and customer engagement campaigns to improve low-value customer activity.

##  Conclusion

The analysis shows that revenue performance is driven by different factors, thats include product category, location, customer segment and customer spending behaviour. Mobile Phones and Computing are major revenue contributors, while Accessories offer stronger profit margins. Nairobi is the leading market, and Corporate customers deliver the strongest segment-level profit margin.

The business should focus on improving customer retention, expanding opportunities in lower-performing cities, protecting high-value customer relationships and using monthly sales trends to plan stock and promotional activities.
